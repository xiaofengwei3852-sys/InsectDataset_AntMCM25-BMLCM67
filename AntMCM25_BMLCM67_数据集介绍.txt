AntMCM25 与 BMLCM67 的构建说明
Origins of AntMCM25 and BMLCM67

中文
论文为研究同一昆虫目内的细粒度分类，从 GBIF 在线收集图像，构建了两个物种级数据集。

AntMCM25：包含膜翅目蚁科的 25 个蚂蚁物种、13,603 张图像，关注红火蚁等外观相似物种的识别。

BMLCM67：包含鳞翅目的 67 个蝶蛾物种、68,793 张图像，涉及农林昆虫，关注不同生活阶段的形态差异及相似翅纹带来的识别困难。

两者均呈长尾分布，用于研究细粒度分类与类别不平衡问题。

English
The paper constructed two species-level datasets from images collected online via GBIF to study fine-grained classification within insect orders.

AntMCM25 contains 25 ant species in Formicidae, Hymenoptera, with 13,603 images, focusing on visually similar species such as fire ants.

BMLCM67 contains 67 butterfly and moth species in Lepidoptera, with 68,793 images, including agricultural and forestry insects with varied life stages and similar wing patterns.

Both have long-tailed distributions for studying fine-grained recognition and class imbalance. Counts follow the paper.
